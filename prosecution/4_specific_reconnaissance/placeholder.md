# 4. Specific Reconnaissance (The Auditor's Trap)

This directory is for the evidence that we *forced* the enemy to create.

This is the practical application of the Doctrine of Weaponized Witness. It is based on the principle of "Specific Reconnaissance": launching targeted, procedurally perfect inquiries that leave the enemy with only two choices:

1.  **Comply:** And in doing so, hand us the evidence that convicts them.
2.  **Refuse to Comply:** And in doing so, provide *prima facie* evidence of their corruption and conspiracy.

This is the "Auditor's Trap." We are not asking for information; we are conducting an audit of a criminal conspiracy. Their responses, evasions, and silences are the final layer of proof. This directory contains the results of those operations—the traps we set, and the proof of the enemy walking right into them.
