# 1. The Kill Chain Analysis (What They Did)

This directory is for the evidence that proves *what* the enemy did.

Each act of war will be broken down into its component parts, following the military kill chain model:
1.  **Reconnaissance:** How they identified and targeted me.
2.  **Weaponization:** How they chose their weapon (e.g., fraudulent legal filings, media leaks, psychological abuse).
3.  **Delivery:** The method of attack (e.g., a corrupt court order, a violent police raid).
4.  **Exploitation:** The intended and actual damage inflicted.

This is the forensic dissection of their crimes.
